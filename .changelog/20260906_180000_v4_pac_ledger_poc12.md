## Why

exp_28 showed the substrate cannot hold structure; its ledger showed why. The SEC pressure's work
over a baseline run is 670,000–1,140,000 against a binding energy of ~63,000, and the design pass
located the source exactly: at fixed entropy the pressure is already the gradient of a pair energy,
and the whole non-conservation is the entropy ratchet — pair energy created by entropy growth
(689,700) accounts for the pressure work (667,300). In the house's terms the substrate had no
ledger. This round adds one.

## Instruments (stay)

- `sec_pair_energy`: the shifted kernel `K = sec·r0·(e^{−r/r0} − e^{−2})`, `E_SEC`, `∂E_SEC/∂S_i`; the
  force is its gradient at fixed entropy (finite difference, float64, 1.6e-7).
- `pac_kappa`: a per-particle potential budget `P(0) = κ|U_grav(0)| m_i/Σm`; entropy growth pays
  `(∂E/∂S)ΔS` and is clipped to what the budget can pay; decay repays; transfer identity exact
  (9e-10). `PACLedger` reports `sec_energy_int`, `budget_int`, `budget_frac`, `total_pac`,
  `closure_pac`; engine bounds `budget_bound_frac_max`, `budget_exhausted_tick`.
- `pac_kappa=None` bit-identical to the derived-sink code (the inert path reproduces exp_28's
  recorded B0 seed-1 marks to 0.00); κ = 0 is gravity-only bit for bit; severed budgets frozen.
- `tests/v4` 27 → 34 (fail-first); suite 176.

## POC-12

Gates 11/11; budget calibration (κ 0.25–16, no structure metric by construction); one-arm runner;
aggregator with the random-field floor; grid driver. Proxy grid 15 runs (κ ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 2, ∞} ×
3 seeds), n = 4000 grid 15 runs.

**Result 3/4 at both sizes, kill fires by the letter.** The ledger works: bound (KE/|U_grav|
0.49–0.54 at κ = 0.5 vs 10.7–22.3 unbounded), total conserved, transfer exact, budget binding, the
web surviving (0.51–0.76 proxy, 0.44–0.78 at n = 4000, vs 0.03–0.10; 4.8–5.7 σ) — the relaxation
oscillator is gone. Against gravity alone the registered arm wins 2/3 on the proxy (0.2 σ) and 3/3
at n = 4000 by 1.1 σ against a 2 σ bar: T1 fails, the mapping retired as the object that holds
structure beyond gravity at κ = 0.5 with the proxy deciding. Unscored, post hoc: at n = 4000 κ = 1
holds 0.70–0.80, above gravity in every seed by 2.8 σ, and κ = 2 collapses; on the proxy, whose
pressure range exceeds half its box, the engine never adds. The virial prediction held in all six
seeds. **Next: R1b** — κ = 1 at n = 4000 on fresh seeds with the box-to-range ratio declared; the
pressure's form stays a candidate behind it; R2 runs on a substrate that is actually bound.
