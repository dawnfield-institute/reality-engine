  spacing of |S_i − S_j| > τ` (Milestone R exp_15 form); severed particles drift, interact with

- **`LandauerErasure`**: released entropy costs `LN2·|ΔS|` of kinetic energy (fracton's constant,
  named). Inert unless `landauer`. Exploratory arm; ~1% of the pressure work.

## POC-11 (`proof_of_concepts/v4/poc_11_derived_sink/`)

construction), one-arm runner (`exp_03`: B0/S/D/R/L/SL), aggregator applying the registered frame
(`exp_04`), grid driver (`run_grid.sh`). Proxy grid 36 runs (6 s each), n = 4000 grid 12 runs.

entropy field, which on this substrate are the collapse cores; severance removes the bound,
connected part and leaves the rest hotter and *less* connected than a random subset at the same

leaves and S = R); random removal at the onset τ holds more; the matched drag holds as much or more.

rule (KE/|U| 11–18, not 960) and it still cannot hold. **C4.1 option 3 closed; option 2 (derive the
pressure term from the SEC functional) remains.** Journal
`journals/2026-09-05_the-trigger-selects-the-cores.md`.
